# AI Usage Policy

_Standard [contribution](https://github.com/radiocosmology/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) rules apply to all AI usage_.

The `radiocosmology` project has specific rules for AI usage:

- **All AI usage must be disclosed**. Pull requests should state the tool that was used and
  the extent to which the work was assisted by AI.
- **You must fully understand your code**. Do not create a pull request unless you can explain
  what your changes do and why without the help of an AI agent.
- **Issues and discussions can use AI assistance but must have a human entirely in the loop**. Any
  content generated by an AI must be reviewed and edited by a human before submission. AI is often
  either too verbose or too concise. Humans must ensure that the correct point is being made, rather
  than offloading that effort onto reviewers (or reviewing agents).
- **No AI-generated media**. Only text and code generated by AI are allowed. Art, images, videos,
  audio, etc... are explicitly banned.

Please remember that this entire project is maintained by humans. Every issue and pull request is read and
reviewed by humans (and sometimes other agents). It is not acceptable to submit low-effort, sloppy work,
since it puts the burden of validation on the maintainers.
